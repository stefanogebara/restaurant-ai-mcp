# 📱 No-Show Prevention System - Staff Training Guide

## What is This?

The dashboard now shows a **risk score** for each reservation. This score (0-100) predicts how likely a customer is to not show up. The higher the score, the higher the no-show risk.

Think of it like a weather forecast: it tells you the **probability** of something happening so you can prepare.

---

## 🎯 Why This Matters

**Empty tables = lost revenue.**

- A no-show during Friday dinner means that table sits empty during peak hours
- You can't fill it last-minute because other customers already made plans
- Average loss per no-show: **€50** (or more for larger parties)

**With this system:**
- Spot high-risk reservations BEFORE they no-show
- Take simple actions (call, SMS, deposit) that dramatically reduce no-shows
- Turn 80-90% of potential no-shows into confirmed arrivals

---

## 🚦 Risk Levels (What They Mean)

### 🟢 LOW RISK (0-24 points)
**What it means**: Customer is very likely to show up.
**What to do**: Nothing! Just provide excellent service as usual.

**Example**: Local regular customer, small party, booked a few days ahead

---

### 🟡 MEDIUM RISK (25-49 points)
**What it means**: Some risk factors, but nothing alarming.
**What to do**: Consider automated SMS reminder a few hours before.

**Example**: First-time customer, weeknight, moderate party size

---

### 🟠 HIGH RISK (50-74 points)
**What it means**: Multiple risk factors. Elevated no-show probability.
**What to do**: **Call customer 24 hours before** to confirm they're still coming.

**Example**: Large party, booked weeks ahead, weekend prime-time

**Script for confirmation call**:
> "Hi [Name], this is [Restaurant]. I'm calling to confirm your reservation for [party size] on [date] at [time]. Are you still able to join us?"

**Takes 2 minutes. Reduces no-shows by 60-70%.**

---

### 🔴 VERY HIGH RISK (75-100 points)
**What it means**: Strong likelihood of no-show. Multiple red flags.
**What to do**: **Require credit card deposit** (€10-20/person) OR make confirmation call.

**Example**: New customer, very large party (8+), far-future booking, tourist

**How to request deposit**:
> "For large parties, we require a credit card to hold the reservation. There's no charge unless you cancel within 24 hours or don't show up. Can I take your card details?"

**Reduces no-shows by 80-90%.**

## 📊 Understanding the Scores

The system looks at **11 different factors**:

### ❌ Things that INCREASE risk (red flags):
- **First-time customer** (+30): No track record with us
- **Large party** (+15-25): Harder to coordinate, people drop out
- **Booked weeks ahead** (+20): Plans change, they forget, make other arrangements
- **Short notice** (+15): Impulsive booking, plans may change
- **Weekend prime-time** (+15): Fri/Sat 7-9 PM has highest no-show rates
- **No email contact** (+10): Phone-only = less commitment
- **Tourist** (+15): Travel plans are uncertain
- **Language barrier** (+10): Miscommunication risk

### ✅ Things that DECREASE risk (green flags):
- **Same-day urgent** (-10): They're coming NOW, highly committed!
- **Loyal customer** (-10): Excellent track record
- **Special occasion** (-10): Birthday/Anniversary = high commitment
- **Dietary restrictions** (-5): Shows planning and intentionality
- **Local customer** (-5): More reliable than tourists

## ❓ Common Questions

### "What if the prediction is wrong?"
That's fine! The system aims for 80-85% accuracy, not 100%. It's better to occasionally call a reliable customer than to miss a real no-show.

### "Won't customers be offended by deposit requests?"
No! Frame it professionally: "For large parties, we hold tables with a credit card - no charge unless you cancel within 24 hours." This is industry standard.

### "Do I HAVE to follow the recommendations?"
No, they're **recommendations**. Use your judgment. But the data shows these actions reduce no-shows by 60-90%.

### "How does it know if someone is a tourist?"
When customers book, they can select "Tourist" or "Local". The system also looks at phone area codes and booking patterns.

### "Will it learn over time?"
Yes! As we track actual no-shows vs predictions, the system will get smarter and more accurate for OUR specific restaurant.
